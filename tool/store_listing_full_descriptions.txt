Every trade, every result, every emotion — mapped in one place. Track your progress, master your mindset, and eliminate uncertainty. Your home screen shows what matters before the session starts.

PRE-TRADE CHECKLIST
Follow your checklist, build your routine, and watch your progress grow in real time. Custom sections, scheduled reminders, and daily validation — stay disciplined session after session.

MY STRATEGY
Take control of every trade. Define your strategy, risk parameters, trading sessions, rules, and setup models in one structured workspace. Risk management and schedule compliance built in.
